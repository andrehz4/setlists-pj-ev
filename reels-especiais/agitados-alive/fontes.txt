FONTES DOS DROPS (reel Alive)

1. VOCÊ SABIA? "Dollar Short", fita demo do Stone Gossard
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alive_(Pearl_Jam_song) (seção Origin). Trecho: a música "originated as an instrumental titled 'Dollar Short'", numa fita demo (Stone Gossard Demos '91).
   Referência citada: Nina Pearlman, "Black Days", Guitar World, dezembro de 2002.

2. A LETRA: mãe do Eddie contou que o pai era o padrasto
   Mesma página da Wikipedia (seção Lyrics). Referência citada: Cameron Crowe, "Five Against the World", Rolling Stone, 28/10/1993
   (https://web.archive.org/web/20070619084803/http://www.rollingstone.com/news/story/10560431/five_against_the_world).
   Trecho: "his mother told him that his father was actually his stepfather".

3. O SINGLE, 1991: lançado em 7 de julho de 1991, 51 dias antes do Ten
   Wikipedia (seção Release / lead): "released ... 51 days before the release of the band's debut album, Ten". Data cita pearljam.com
   (https://pearljam.com/music/album/alive).

4. LOLLAPALOOZA, 1992: citação de Perry Farrell (co-fundador do festival)
   https://www.loudersound.com/bands-artists/lollapalooza-uncensored-story (Tom Beaujour e Richard Bienstock, Louder, 18/06/2025).
   Trecho verbatim: "I remember Eddie did a second-storey dive off the speaker stacks into the crowd. I thought, for sure this guy broke something."
   Tradução livre no drop. A fonte fala do Lollapalooza 92 em geral; o drop não afirma que seja este salto exato.
   Clipe do acervo: salto-lollapalooza-1992-01 (show identificado como Lollapalooza 1992 no clips.json, origem https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O4gpF6VmaRc).

5. EDDIE VEDDER, 2006: "O público mudou o significado pra mim. Levantaram a maldição."
   Wikipedia (Lyrics) citando Jonathan Cohen, Billboard, "Pearl Jam Tells Its 'Story' At VH1 Taping"
   (https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/pearl-jam-tells-its-story-at-vh1-taping-58253/). Trechos: "It was a curse" e "They lifted the curse. The audience changed the meaning for me."
   Confirmado também por busca (Radio X: https://www.radiox.co.uk/features/pearl-jam-alive-story-meaning-lyrics/ ).

6. O CLIPE, 1991: ao vivo no RKCNDY, Seattle; citação do Jeff Ament
   Wikipedia (Music video): gravado em 3/8/1991 no RKCNDY, Seattle; Ament (Rolling Stone, 1993, mesma matéria de Crowe):
   "Sonically, it's an inferior version of the song. But it's live."

LEGENDA, fatos extras:
 - Eddie era segurança em San Diego, recebeu a fita e escreveu a letra antes de ir surfar (Wikipedia, Origin, cita Crowe/Rolling Stone 1993).
 - "Mamasan", trilogia com Once e Footsteps (Wikipedia, cita Crowe).
 - Solo final de Mike McCready gravado em junho de 1991 na mixagem; Tim Palmer: "There was no piecing together to do; it was one take." (Wikipedia, cita Guitar World 2002).
 - Mike McCready disse que "copiou" o solo de Ace Frehley em "She" (Wikipedia, cita Guitar School 1995). Não usado na legenda.
