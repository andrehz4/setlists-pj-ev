FONTES DE PORCH (cada drop e cada afirmação da legenda)

1. Drop "VOCÊ SABIA?" (faixa 8 do Ten, 1991; letra e música de Eddie Vedder)
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_(Pearl_Jam_album) , lista de faixas: "title8 = Porch | music8 = Vedder | length8 = 3:30";
   letras do disco todas de Vedder ("all_lyrics = Vedder"). Lançamento: 27 de agosto de 1991 (Epic).

2. Drop "COMO NASCEU"
   https://www.songfacts.com/facts/pearl-jam/porch : "One of the earliest Pearl Jam songs, lead singer Eddie Vedder wrote
   "Porch" on his way up from San Diego to meet the rest of the band for the first time."

3. Drop "SAIR DA VARANDA" (leitura, apresentada como leitura e não como fato)
   https://americansongwriter.com/4-songs-you-didnt-know-eddie-vedder-wrote-solo-for-pearl-jam/ : "about doing much in the
   world, about getting off one's porch and entering the fray of the real world"; "Written by Eddie Vedder".
   Songfacts (mesma URL do item 2): "very much open to interpretation, as Eddie Vedder intended".

4. Drop "MOORE THEATRE, 1990"
   https://www.setlist.fm/setlist/pearl-jam/1990/the-moore-theatre-seattle-wa-7bd6e6e4.html : Moore Theatre, Seattle,
   22 de dezembro de 1990, "Porch" é a 7a música do setlist.

5. Drop "DROP IN THE PARK, 1992" (momento forte)
   Imagens: acervo media/reels-clips/agitados/clips.json (drop-in-the-park-seattle-1992-01/02/03, "Eddie pendurado na treliça
   do teto do palco"), origem https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AumGmvOHr_4 (título "Pearl Jam - Drop in the Park 1992").
   Duração: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_(Pearl_Jam_album) , seção "Drop in the Park LP", lado 4: "Porch | length1 = 12:42"
   (no disco, 3:30). O texto NÃO diz que o Eddie estava pendurado durante Porch, só que está pendurado no show.

6. Drop "MTV UNPLUGGED, 1992"
   https://ultimatepearljam.com/pearl-jam-unplugged/ : "the climactic moment, which aired last but came in the middle of the
   actual taping, was "Porch." ... Vedder stands atop his stool writing "PRO CHOICE!!!" with a marker before leaping back to
   the floor during the song's long jam." Songfacts (item 2): "he wrote "Pro Choice" in his arm when Pearl Jam performed it
   on their MTV Unplugged concert in 1992". Gravação: 16 de março de 1992, Kaufman Astoria Studios, Queens
   (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Unplugged_(Pearl_Jam_album)).
